title: Report a suspicious email, and what to do if you clicked a link
category: security
keywords: phishing, suspicious email, scam, spam, clicked link, entered password, attachment, fraud, fake login page, QR code, text message, phone call asking for password, pretending to be IT, gift cards
---
If you have not clicked anything: use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook, then delete the email. Do not forward it to colleagues.

If you clicked a link, opened an attachment or entered your password:
1. Change your password straight away from a device you trust.
2. Tell the service desk now. We will check your account sign-ins and may need to reset your MFA.
3. If an attachment asked you to "enable content", disconnect the laptop from the network and leave it switched on so it can be investigated.

Reporting quickly is always right. You will not be in trouble for reporting a mistake.
