id: KB003
title: Your account is locked out
category: access
keywords: locked, locked out, account disabled, too many attempts, sign-in blocked, account locked, wrong password too many times
---
Accounts lock for 15 minutes after several wrong password attempts.

1. Wait 15 minutes, then try again carefully. Check Caps Lock and the keyboard language.
2. If it keeps locking, a device may be trying an old password in the background. Update the password on your work phone's email app, any mapped drives and saved Wi-Fi details, or remove and re-add the work account on the phone.
3. If the account says "disabled" rather than "locked", it may have been switched off after a long absence or a role change. The service desk will check with your manager before re-enabling it.
