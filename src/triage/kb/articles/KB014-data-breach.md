---
id: KB014
title: Personal data sent to the wrong person
category: security
keywords: data breach, wrong recipient, sent in error, personal data, GDPR, information governance, mistake
---
If personal data has gone to the wrong person, report it the same day: the organisation may have 72 hours to tell the ICO.

1. Do not delete anything. Note who it went to, what was in it, and when.
2. Ask the recipient to delete it and confirm in writing that they have done so.
3. Report it to the service desk and to your information governance or data protection team today. They decide within 72 hours whether it must be reported to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
4. Tell your manager.
