If a laptop will not turn on or gets stuck while starting, try these steps before raising a ticket.

1. Plug in the charger and leave it for 10 minutes, then hold the power button for 15 seconds and try again.
2. Disconnect docks, USB sticks and external screens, then start the laptop.
3. If it asks for a BitLocker recovery key, call the service desk. We will verify your identity and read you the key. Never post a recovery key in a ticket.
4. Frequent blue screens can mean a hardware fault. Note the stop code shown on the screen.
