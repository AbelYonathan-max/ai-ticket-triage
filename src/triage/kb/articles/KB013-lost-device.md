Report a lost or stolen device as soon as you notice, even if it might turn up.

1. Tell the service desk the device type, when and where you last had it, and whether it was locked.
2. We will block the device and can remotely wipe a phone or laptop.
3. If it was stolen, report it to the police and send us the crime reference number.
4. A lost smartcard must be cancelled before a new one is issued.

Devices are encrypted, but reporting quickly reduces the risk of a personal data breach.
