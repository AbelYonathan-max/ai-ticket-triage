id: KB009
title: Get access to a shared mailbox
category: m365
keywords: shared mailbox, team inbox, send as, send on behalf, generic mailbox, full access, mailbox access
---
Shared mailbox access needs approval from the mailbox owner, usually the team manager.

1. Raise a request naming the mailbox and whether you need to read only, send as the mailbox, or send on behalf of it.
2. Once approved, the mailbox appears in Outlook within about an hour. Restart Outlook if it does not.
3. To send from the mailbox, show the From field (Options > From) and choose the shared address.
