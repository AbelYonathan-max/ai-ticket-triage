New or replacement equipment, such as a monitor, keyboard or laptop, is requested through a hardware request.

1. Raise a hardware request saying what you need and why. Your manager must approve it.
2. Equipment recommended by a display screen equipment (DSE) or occupational health assessment is prioritised. Attach the recommendation.
3. Laptops are replaced on a four to five year cycle, or sooner if faulty.
