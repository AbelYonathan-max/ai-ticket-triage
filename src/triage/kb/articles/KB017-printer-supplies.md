title: Order toner, paper and printer supplies
category: printing
keywords: toner, paper, supplies, cartridge, staples, order
---
Toner for managed printers is ordered automatically when it runs low. If a printer shows "toner low" for more than two days, raise a request with the printer name from the label on the front.

Paper and staples are ordered by each team through the stationery catalogue, not through IT.
