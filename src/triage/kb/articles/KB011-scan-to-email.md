title: Scanning to email or to a folder
category: printing
keywords: scan, scanner, scan to email, scan to folder, SMTP error, blank scans
---
Scan to email sends scans to your own work address only, and large scans can fail.

1. Scan to email only sends to your own work address. Check your Junk folder.
2. Large scans (over about 20MB) can fail. Scan in black and white or split the document into smaller parts.
3. Blank pages usually mean the document was placed face down in the feeder or face up on the glass. Check the orientation shown on the printer.
4. Scan to folder destinations are set up per team. Raise a request with the folder path you need.
