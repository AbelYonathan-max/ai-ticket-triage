title: Teams audio, video and screen sharing problems
category: m365
keywords: Teams, audio, microphone, camera, screen share, call dropping, meeting, headset
---
Most Teams call problems come from the wrong audio or camera device, or another app using the camera.

1. In Teams, go to Settings > Devices and check the right speaker, microphone and camera are selected. Make a test call.
2. Close other apps that might be using the camera, such as Zoom or the Camera app.
3. If screen sharing shows black to others, share a single window instead of the whole screen and make sure Teams is up to date.
4. Calls that drop for a whole floor at once are usually network problems. Report the location and time.
