You can reset your own password at any time with self-service password reset.

1. On another device, go to the self-service password reset page linked from the sign-in screen ("Can't access your account?").
2. Enter your work email address and complete the verification step (the Authenticator app or a text to your registered phone).
3. Choose a new password. It must be at least 14 characters and must not be one you have used before. A short phrase of three random words works well.
4. On a Windows laptop, connect to the network (in the office or on the VPN) and lock then unlock the device so it picks up the new password.

If you have never registered for self-service reset, the service desk can reset your password after checking your identity. We will never ask you to tell us your current password.
