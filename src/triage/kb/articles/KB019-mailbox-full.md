When your mailbox is full you cannot send email, so clear space or move old email to your online archive.

1. Empty the Deleted Items folder: right-click it and choose Empty Folder.
2. Find the biggest email with View > Sort by Size, then delete what you do not need or save the attachments to your files first.
3. Move old email to your online archive. It is the "In-Place Archive" folder in Outlook and does not count towards your mailbox size. Email older than two years moves there automatically each week.
4. If your mailbox is still full after this, raise a ticket and say how full it is (File > Info shows the size). Do not set up rules that forward work email to a personal account.
