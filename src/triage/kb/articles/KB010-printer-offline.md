title: Printer is offline or prints do not come out
category: printing
keywords: printer, offline, not printing, print release, follow-me, card, queue, stuck jobs
---
If a printer shows as offline or your print does not come out, check the printer itself and how secure print works.

1. Check the printer screen for an error such as a paper jam, empty tray or toner warning.
2. With secure print (follow-me printing), jobs only print when you tap your ID card at a printer. Jobs wait for 24 hours.
3. If your card says "user not found", register the card again at the printer using your network login.
4. If the printer shows offline only on your laptop, remove it and add it again from the print server.
5. If a job is stuck at the top of the queue and blocking others, tell us the printer name. We can clear the queue remotely.
