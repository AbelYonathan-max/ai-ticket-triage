title: Fix VPN connection problems when working remotely
category: network
keywords: VPN, remote access, GlobalProtect, AnyConnect, gateway, certificate, working from home
---
Most VPN problems are fixed by checking your home internet and reconnecting the VPN client.

1. Check your home internet works by opening a public website.
2. Disconnect and reconnect the VPN client, then restart the laptop if it still fails.
3. If you see a certificate error, connect the laptop to the office network once (or bring it to a hub) so it can renew its device certificate.
4. Hotel and public Wi-Fi often block VPNs until you accept their terms in a browser. Open a browser first, accept the terms, then connect the VPN.
5. If the VPN connects but you cannot reach shared drives, sign out of Windows and back in so drive mappings refresh.

Tell us the exact error message and the time it happened. That helps us find your session in the logs.
