title: Outlook is not syncing, sending or opening
category: m365
keywords: Outlook, email, disconnected, outbox, not receiving, crashing, password prompts, sync
---
If Outlook is not sending, receiving or opening properly, check webmail first to see whether the problem is the app or the mailbox.

1. Check whether webmail (Outlook on the web) shows the missing emails. If it does, the problem is the Outlook app, not your mailbox.
2. Close Outlook fully, then reopen it. If it crashes on start, start it in safe mode (hold Ctrl while opening) and remove recently added add-ins.
3. Large attachments can block the outbox. Move a stuck email to Drafts, remove or resize the attachment, then send again.
4. Repeated password prompts usually clear after signing out of Office (File > Account > Sign out) and signing back in.

If nobody can send or receive email, report it as an incident with the time it started.
