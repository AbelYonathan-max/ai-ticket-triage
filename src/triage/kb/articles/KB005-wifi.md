title: Connect to the staff Wi-Fi
category: network
keywords: wifi, wi-fi, wireless, no internet, connected but no internet, staff network, guest wifi
---
Use the staff Wi-Fi network for work devices; it signs you in automatically with your laptop's certificate.

1. Choose the staff network, not the guest network. Your laptop connects automatically with its device certificate.
2. If it says "connected, no internet", turn Wi-Fi off and on, or forget the network and reconnect.
3. If you changed your password recently, sign out of Windows and back in while plugged into a dock, so the laptop updates its details.
4. If several people in the same area have problems, tell us the floor and room. It may be an access point fault.

Visitors should use the guest network with a code from reception.
