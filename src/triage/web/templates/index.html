How it works

  1. Redact. Names, emails, phone, NHS and National Insurance numbers, card numbers, IP addresses and passwords are removed first. Only redacted text is stored or sent to AI.
  2. Classify. A machine learning model predicts the category, incident or request, impact and urgency, and shows the words behind each prediction.
  3. Apply safety rules. Rules for security incidents, data breaches, outages and patient safety can raise the priority but never lower it.
  4. Prioritise. The ITIL impact and urgency matrix gives P1 to P4 and the SLA response and resolution times, counting service desk hours and bank holidays.
  5. Assist and check. An optional Azure OpenAI step drafts a reply from approved help articles. Low confidence, P1 and risky tickets go to an analyst.
